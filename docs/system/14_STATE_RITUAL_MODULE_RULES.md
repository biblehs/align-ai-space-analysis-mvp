# State Ritual Module Rules

## Purpose

ALIGN should not stop at space actions alone.  
For bedroom sleep scenarios especially, the product should help the user feel that the room's change has landed in the body and not just on the surface.

This module exists to bridge:

1. **Space shift**
2. **State shift**
3. **Felt change**

The module is not generic wellness advice. It is a small transition step that follows the room action.

## Where it belongs

### Snapshot

Recommended order:

1. Type
2. Reading
3. Evidence
4. State summary
5. Tonight's Shift
6. **Tonight's Rest Ritual**
7. **What you may feel tonight**
8. Unlock Full Report

### Full Report

Recommended placement:

- after the practical action plan
- before the closing reflection or CTA

## What the module must do

### 1. Stop escalation

The first line should release pressure.

It should tell the user:

- you do not need to keep optimizing tonight
- this step is enough for now
- pause before doing more

### 2. Give one small state action

The ritual should be:

- real
- short
- achievable tonight
- tied to the space shift that just happened

Examples:

- put the phone down for ten minutes
- leave only one soft light on
- sit at the bed edge and breathe slowly
- drink a warm cup of water or tea
- stand by the window for a moment

### 3. Translate action into felt effect

The module should explain why this matters in state terms:

- let the room's change land in the body
- let the day end more clearly
- reduce the feeling that something is still happening
- help the room hold rest a little more clearly

### 4. Invite observation, not promises

`What you may feel tonight` should:

- name one to three subtle changes
- avoid dramatic claims
- sound observant, not performative

Examples:

- it may feel a little easier to slow down once you get into bed
- the room may feel less unfinished than it did before
- you may notice that the space feels more like an ending than another task

## Hard rules

- The ritual must follow directly from the current room action.
- Give one main ritual only, with at most one light alternative.
- Do not turn the module into generic health or self-care advice.
- Do not require purchases, products, or special objects.
- Do not promise sleep, healing, or emotional resolution.
- Keep the module shorter and softer than the action block itself.

## What this module is not

It is not:

- a health tip list
- a meditation lesson
- a life-coaching insert
- a mystical energy explanation

It is:

- the final step that helps a room action become a felt state shift

## Product value

Without this module, ALIGN can feel like:

- analysis
- diagnosis
- advice

With this module, ALIGN begins to feel like:

- analysis
- guidance
- landing
- small emotional support

This is an important bridge between trust-building and later commerce.  
If the user can feel a real shift tonight, they are more likely to believe in:

- progress scans
- reinforcement suggestions
- support objects
- ritual-oriented room upgrades
