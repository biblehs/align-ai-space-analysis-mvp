## Snapshot Support Module

### Eyebrow

Make tonight easier

### Title

A softer last light

### Description

You do not need to finish the whole room tonight. If you want the room to help you slow down more easily, start by adding one softer final layer of light near the bed.

### Why this fits your room

When a bedroom already feels visually light but still does not fully settle into rest, the missing piece is often not more decor. It is a softer last light that helps the room leave "still in use" mode.

- Find a softer light for this room

## Primary Support

### Night Wind-Down Support

A softer final layer that helps the room leave "still in use" mode.

#### Why this matters here

Your room may already feel calm and visually light, but that is not the same as feeling clearly held by night. A lower, softer light helps the room shift out of functional use and into a more settled end-of-day rhythm.

#### What it helps shift

- from functional to softer
- from bright to winding down
- from still active to ready for rest

#### Best for

Rooms that feel calm, but still a little on at night.
