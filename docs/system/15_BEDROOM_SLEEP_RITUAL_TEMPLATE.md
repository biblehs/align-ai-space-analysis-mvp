# Bedroom / Sleep Ritual Template

## Use case

Use this module when:

- room type is `bedroom`
- user goal is `sleep` or `rest`
- the room action is focused on clearing the bed, softening evening light, lowering alertness, or building a clearer rest cue

## Final template

### Module title

**Tonight's Rest Ritual**

### Body

After you lighten the bed zone, stop there for tonight.  
Put your phone down for ten minutes, keep only one softer light on, and sit at the bed or by the window long enough to let the room quiet with you.

The goal is not to do more, but to let the room's shift land in your body.

### Felt shift title

**What you may feel tonight**

### Felt shift body

You may notice that the room feels a little less unfinished, or that it becomes easier to slow down once you get into bed.

You do not need a dramatic difference right away.  
Just notice whether the room feels slightly more able to hold the end of the day.

## Shorter alternate version

### Module title

**Let this step land**

### Body

Once the bed area feels lighter, stop there for tonight.  
Leave one soft light on, put your phone down, and give yourself two quiet minutes in the room you just reset.

### Felt shift title

**What you may feel tonight**

### Felt shift body

The room may feel a little less active, and it may be easier to believe that the day is actually ending.

## Why this template works

- It follows the room action instead of replacing it
- It reduces task pressure instead of adding more
- It uses real, believable actions
- It creates a bridge from physical change to emotional change
- It helps ALIGN feel supportive without drifting into generic wellness language

## Do not turn it into

- a sleep hygiene checklist
- a spiritual ritual script
- a health lecture
- a shopping recommendation block

## Good adjacent lines

These lines can support the module nearby:

- `Tonight, the point is not more effort. It is a clearer ending.`
- `Let the room become quieter, and let yourself follow it.`
- `You are not trying to perfect the room tonight. You are letting it soften enough to hold rest.`
