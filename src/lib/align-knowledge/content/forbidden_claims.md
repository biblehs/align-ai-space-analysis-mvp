# Forbidden Claims

The ALIGN knowledge system must not authorize the writer layer to produce:

- medical diagnosis language
- trauma interpretation
- psychiatric or therapeutic claims
- energy-field or chakra language
- guaranteed cause-and-effect statements from a single room photo
- absolute claims about color psychology or personality
- healing language presented as if it were evidence
- emotional certainty claims that outrun the visible room signals

Use:

- visible tension
- restorative cues
- support signal
- more settled
- more held
- more alert than intended
- feeling more able to rest
- a clearer cue for rest

Avoid:

- diagnosis
- trauma
- healing aura
- blocked energy field
- frequency
- hormonal cure
- "the room is healing you"
- "the space is clearing your energy"
