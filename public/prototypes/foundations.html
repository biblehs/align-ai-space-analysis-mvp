Color System

Warm minimal, not generic minimal.

ALIGN keeps its warm-white, sage, and soft editorial feel, but uses them with more restraint: fewer heavy modules, clearer lines, and more deliberate contrast.

Canvas Warm#fcfcfb · default page background
Ink Warm#2f2e2c · headline and action ink
Sage Primary#8a9a86 · signals, pills, subtle emphasis
Secondary Warm#eeeae4 · soft fills and editorials
Surface Warm#f4efe8 · story sections
Surface Sand#f5f0eb · warm highlights
